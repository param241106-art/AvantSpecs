AVANTSPECS — WEBSITE USERS / TARGET AUDIENCE
=====================================================
This site is B2B — there are no end-consumer shoppers. Every visitor is
either a business buyer, a partner, or internal staff. Below are the
user types the site is designed for.

1. PRIMARY USER: OVERSEAS WHOLESALE BUYER / IMPORTER
------------------------------------------------------
Who they are:
- Procurement managers, buyers, or owners at companies that import
  essential oils and oleoresins in bulk (25kg to multiple metric tons)
- Based in North America, Europe, Middle East (UAE/Saudi Arabia), Asia
  Pacific (Australia/Singapore), or Latin America
- Represent industries such as: cosmetics/personal care manufacturing,
  nutraceutical/supplement production, flavor & fragrance houses,
  pharmaceutical formulators, food & beverage manufacturers

What they come to do:
- Browse the Product Register to see available SKUs, specs, and origin
- Check certifications/compliance documentation before committing to a
  supplier (FSSAI, ISO, HACCP, GMP, USFDA, Kosher, Halaal)
- Submit an RFQ (Request for Quote) via the multi-step Order Portal,
  specifying product, volume, incoterm, and destination port
- Confirm lead times and shipping logistics to their region
- Get a document checklist (COA, TDS, SDS, COO, Phytosanitary, etc.)
  for customs clearance in their country

Needs from the site:
- Fast way to filter/search products (by name or Latin binomial)
- Clear MOQ and COA availability per product
- Trust signals: certifications, documented process, real leadership
- Low-friction quote request (guided steps rather than one long form)
- Confidence that paperwork/compliance is handled for their market

2. SECONDARY USER: SAMPLE / SMALL-BATCH BUYER
------------------------------------------------------
- Smaller brands, formulators, or first-time buyers testing a product
  before committing to bulk volume
- Uses the "Sample (<1kg)" or "Small (1–25kg)" volume options in the
  RFQ portal
- More likely to read the FAQ section (MOQ, sample policy, lead times)
  before submitting an enquiry

3. TERTIARY USER: EXISTING / RETURNING TRADE PARTNER
------------------------------------------------------
- A buyer who already has a relationship with AvantSpecs and returns to:
  - Check new-crop availability or market notes (footer newsletter)
  - Request specs on an additional product via "Request Specs" links
  - Reach the trade desk directly by phone/WhatsApp or email

4. INTERNAL / OPERATIONAL USER: THE AVANTSPECS TRADE DESK
------------------------------------------------------
- Receives RFQ portal submissions and contact form submissions (via
  Formspree-forwarded emails)
- Uses the generated reference number (e.g. AVS-2026-XXXXX) to track
  and respond to each enquiry within a stated 48-hour window
- Represented on-site by 3 named leadership/trade desk contacts

5. CASUAL / RESEARCH VISITOR
------------------------------------------------------
- Journalists, students, or competitors researching the essential oil
  export trade, regional certifications, or supply chain documentation
- Typically only reads About/Trade & Markets sections, does not convert

DEVICE & CONTEXT NOTES
------------------------------------------------------
- Buyers may browse on desktop (office/procurement context) or mobile
  (checking on the go, WhatsApp-linked contact) — site must support both
  via the mobile hamburger nav and responsive product grid
- Buyers are international, so plain, unambiguous English and explicit
  units/incoterms/currency-neutral language matter more than idiom
